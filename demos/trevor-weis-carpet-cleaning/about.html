About

Local, insured, and easy to get hold of

Carpet cleaning done right, by the guy who actually shows up — that's not a slogan, it's the actual business model.

Why this business exists

Carpet cleaning is one of those services where the result is completely obvious. Either a room looks and feels different when you walk back in or it doesn't. That's an easy business to be judged on, which is exactly why it's worth doing properly.

Trevor Weis Carpet Cleaning is locally owned and operated right here in Louisville, KY — one person, one truck, one job at a time. That means every single visit matters more than it would to an outfit with a dozen crews and a call center between you and the person actually doing the work.

It also means being careful about scope. This business cleans carpet, upholstery, and stairs, and does it with the right method for each. It's not going to talk you into a service it isn't set up to do properly.

How the work gets done

  • Quotes go out fast — usually within a business day, and you can get one without an in-person visit.
  • You'll hear up front what will fully lift and what will only lighten.
  • Method and heat get matched to the surface — carpet, upholstery, and stairs are not treated the same way.
  • Fast-dry setup on every job, so rooms and furniture aren't left unusable for days.
  • Fully insured, with a certificate available whenever you want to see it.
