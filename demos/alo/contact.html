What changed

The current contact page lists a mailing address and one gmail.com address. The A la Carte phone number is on a different page entirely. Raffle sales route to an officer's personal outlook.com address, and the donate page tells you a volunteer named Ann will call you back. Every address on this page belongs to a role, so the site keeps working through a board turnover — and the phone number appears in the footer of every page, in the organisation's structured data, and on its Google Business Profile.
