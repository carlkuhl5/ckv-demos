    Renewal, handled

    Memberships carry an expiry date. You get a reminder 30 days out, on the day, and 30 days after — each one a single link that pre-fills your existing record. Nobody has to keep a spreadsheet of who has lapsed.

    Rather not do this online?

    Call (502) 893-5843 or email membership@associationlo.org and the membership chair will take it over the phone.

    What changed

    This is the whole flow: level, details, committees, payment, done, on one URL. Today there are three parallel purchase paths, two of which disagree about what the levels are called, and none of which asks which committee you would like to join — so the membership chair starts every new member with an email asking. PRD Section 07 requires exactly one URL that sells membership, interests captured at join, and no redirect to a second system to pay.
