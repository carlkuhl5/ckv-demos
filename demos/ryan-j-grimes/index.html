Who I am

Blue collar, start to finish.

My dad built kitchens. I grew up around work you can see at the end of the day, and that never left me. Jesus is my foundation, my wife and kids are the reason, and the trade is what I know.

I started Beltway with nothing and no partners. Eight years later it's 55 people and $11 million. None of that happened because I'm smarter than anybody — it happened because we hire A players, hold a high standard, and treat culture like the biggest number on the board.

I serve in Kids Ministry at my church and led youth groups for nine years. Teaching is the part I love most. Coaching contractors is just the same thing in work boots.
