Getting started

What is Common Ground?

Common Ground is a coworking space designed for entrepreneurs, remote professionals, creatives, nonprofits and growing businesses. Whether you need a workspace for the day, a conference room for a meeting, or a long-term business home, Common Ground gives you a professional environment to do your best work.

Where are you located?

We're in Norton Commons at 12100 River Beauty Loop, Prospect, KY 40059 — at the corner of St. Bernadette and River Beauty Loop.

What are your business hours?

Members have 24/7 access to the space, every day of the year. For tours, room rentals and questions, send us a note and we'll get back to you.

Can I tour the space before joining?

Absolutely — we'd love to show you around. Book a tour or get in touch and we'll arrange a visit.

Can I work for just one day?

Yes. Day passes are available for professionals who only need occasional access. Contact us and we'll set you up.

Memberships

What memberships do you offer?

Three, all month-to-month:

  • Side Hustle — 8 coworking day passes per month. Ideal for professionals who need workspace a few times each month.
  • Daily Grind — unlimited coworking access. Great for remote workers, entrepreneurs and small-business owners.
  • Strength in Numbers — a team membership for businesses that need flexible workspace for several people.

Compare all three →

What’s included with my membership?

Every membership includes:

  • High-speed Wi-Fi
  • Complimentary coffee and refreshments
  • Access to shared coworking spaces
  • A professional business environment
  • Networking opportunities and community events
  • Member pricing on conference rooms and event spaces
Do I get my own desk?

Our memberships provide flexible coworking seating rather than assigned desks — sit wherever suits the work that day.

Can I upgrade or change my membership?

Absolutely. You can upgrade as your business grows. Contact our team and we'll help you pick the best option.

Can I bring guests?

Yes. Guests are welcome for meetings and visits. If you plan to bring clients or several guests regularly, get in touch so we can recommend the right membership or meeting-room option.

Billing & cancellation

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Memberships are month-to-month and require 30 days' notice to cancel.

When will I be billed?

Memberships are billed monthly on your recurring billing date.

Is there a cancellation policy?

Yes. We require 30 days' written notice to cancel any membership. After your cancellation request is submitted, one final membership payment is billed to cover the 30-day notice period, and your membership benefits stay active for that time.
