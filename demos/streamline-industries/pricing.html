Straight answers

The questions that come up first.

Does this replace Housecall Pro?

No. Streamline adds to Housecall Pro. Keep your jobs, invoicing and customer records exactly where they are — the Dashboard and the App sit alongside and fill the gaps.

Can I take coaching without the tech?

Yes. Coaching stands on its own at $1,000 or $500 a month. The Dashboard and the App are separate, and you can add either one later.

What are the AI fees on the Dashboard?

The $800 covers the Dashboard. AI usage — mostly the parts ordering doing the legwork for you — is billed on top, based on how much you use it. Ryan walks through real numbers on the call.

Why is there no checkout?

Because the right plan depends on your size, your team and what is actually broken. Fifteen minutes on a call sorts that out faster than a pricing page ever will.
