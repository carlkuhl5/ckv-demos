Endless Second Chances

2 Timothy 2:1 [ Run time ] [ Date ]

Most of us believe in second chances. We get quieter about the fifth one, and by the ninth we have usually found a spiritual way to say no.

Paul tells Timothy to be strengthened by the grace that is in Christ Jesus. Not by discipline, not by resolve, not by having finally gotten his act together. By grace, which by definition is not something he earned on the previous attempt.

This message works through what that means for a church that says it believes in grace and then quietly runs out of it on the people who need the most of it. It is the message Carl comes back to more than any other, and the one people usually stay afterward to talk about.
