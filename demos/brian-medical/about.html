About Brian Medical Transportation

A locally owned, mission-driven NEMT provider dedicated to getting patients where they need to go — safely, on time, every time.

Our Story

Built Around One Simple Belief

Every patient deserves a safe, dignified ride to the care they need — regardless of mobility, income, or how complicated their schedule is. Brian Medical Transportation was founded to serve Louisville, KY families navigating dialysis, cancer treatment, and everyday medical appointments who needed a transportation partner they could count on.

Today, we work directly with patients and families as well as hospital discharge planners, dialysis centers, and senior living communities who trust us with recurring, high-stakes transportation needs.
