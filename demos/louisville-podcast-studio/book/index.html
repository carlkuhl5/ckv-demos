FAQs

Booking questions

How do I book a session?

Send the request form on this page, or call, text or email us. We reply with times that work and confirm the room.

Can I start a membership without booking first?

Yes. Pick a tier on the memberships page and hit Get started — it opens this same form with the tier filled in.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled directly with the team when your session or membership is confirmed. There is no checkout on the site.

Not sure which tier fits?

Send the form and say what you want to make. We will tell you which membership covers it — or whether a single session is enough.
