What changed

Only one year is published today — the 2023 results — and they live at a URL beginning /2022youngartistwinnersalo, three seasons after the fact, occupying most of the homepage. Winners are a record type here. Adding a year is a form; last year's results and their link never move.

Are you a past winner we've missed? The association's records go back further than what is published here. Email youngartists@associationlo.org and we will add you.
Associationof the Louisville Orchestra

The volunteer fundraising arm of the Louisville Orchestra since 1944. Roughly 350 members, about thirty events a year, and more than $2.9 million given to the orchestra.
