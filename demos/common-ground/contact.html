Finding us

Getting here & parking

We're in Norton Commons on River Beauty Loop, at the corner of St. Bernadette. It's about twenty minutes from downtown Louisville and a short drive from Prospect, Goshen and Crestwood.

  • Street parking directly outside on River Beauty Loop
  • Walkable to coffee, lunch and the Norton Commons town centre
  • Members enter with their own credentials, 24 hours a day
  • Visitors and tour guests: come to the front entrance on River Beauty Loop
