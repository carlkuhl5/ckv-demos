Privacy policy

What we collect, why, who can see it, and how long we keep it.

Last updated September 3, 2026. This policy covers associationlo.org and the records the association keeps about members, ticket buyers, donors, raffle entrants, and competition applicants.

What we collect

  • Members — name, email, phone, mailing address, membership tier, join and expiry dates, and the committee interests you select.
  • Ticket buyers — name, email, phone, the guest names and dietary notes you supply for the host, and your order history.
  • Donors — name, email, optional mailing address, amount, designation, and any tribute details you provide.
  • Raffle entrants — name, email, phone, quantity, and issued ticket numbers, as required for a licensed charitable gaming draw.
  • Competition applicants — student and guardian details, school, grade, division, repertoire, teacher, and uploaded files.

Payment information

The association does not receive, process, or store card numbers. All payments are handled by Stripe using fields hosted by Stripe. What reaches our records is the amount, the date, and a Stripe reference — never a card number.

Data about minors

Young Artist Competition applicants are in grades 5–12. Student and guardian records are accessible only to the education committee role, are excluded from every marketing export and mailing list, and are retained on a schedule agreed with the board. Uploaded files are stored against the application and are not distributed by email.

How we use it

To deliver what you asked for — a membership, a seat, a receipt, a competition entry — and to send you what you opted into. Mailing list membership is opt-in and every message carries an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

Nobody, in the sense that matters: the association does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Event hosts receive the guest names and dietary notes for their own event, because that is what the field is for. Service providers who operate the site and process payments receive only what they need to do that.

How long we keep it

Member and order records are kept for as long as required for the association's financial and charitable-gaming reporting obligations. Competition records are retained per the schedule agreed with the board. Mailing list entries are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Email info@associationlo.org or write to the address below.
