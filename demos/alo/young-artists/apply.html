Your application

    Total$0

    $30 per division, charged once, at the point of application. Payment and application can never come apart.

    About the student's data

    Student and guardian records are visible only to the education committee, are excluded from every marketing export, and are retained on a schedule agreed with the board. They are never added to the mailing list.

    Withdrawing

    Refunds for withdrawn entries are issued from the admin by the VP Education — not by asking a volunteer to write a cheque.

    What changed

    Today this is two disconnected steps. The applicant fills a long form, then separately clicks a “Pay Now” button, buys a $30 line item from the same table that sells memberships and annual meeting tickets, and hand-enters a Payment ID back into the form. Entering a second division means filling the entire form again from the top. There is no file upload for repertoire anywhere. This page collects everything once, adds a division for $30 without restarting, takes the files, and ends in a single charge.
