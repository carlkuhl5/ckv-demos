Privacy policy

How Louisville Podcast Studio handles the information you send us through this website.

For the studio to review. This policy was rewritten to match how the rebuilt site actually works. Check it against your own practice, and have it reviewed before launch if you collect anything not described here.

What we collect

We collect only what you give us. If you send the session request form, the newsletter form or a service enquiry, we receive the name, email address, phone number and message you type in. If you call, text or email us directly, we hold that conversation in the studio’s inbox and phone.

We do not ask for payment details on this website and there is no checkout here. Payment for sessions and memberships is arranged with the team directly.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and schedule your session.
  • To run and renew your membership if you start one.
  • To send studio news and updates, but only if you signed up for the newsletter. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except the services we use to run the studio, such as our email provider and scheduling tools.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses privacy-respecting analytics to count page views and see which pages people find useful. That tells us things like how many people opened the memberships page; it does not tell us who you are. No advertising cookies are set by this site.

Recordings and footage

Audio and video recorded in the studio belong to you. We keep a working copy only as long as we need it to finish your edit, and we do not publish anything from your session without your say-so. If you would like your files removed from our systems, ask and we will delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email team@louisvillepodcast.studio and we will handle it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and change the date below.
