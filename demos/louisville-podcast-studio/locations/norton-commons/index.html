Norton Commons Podcast Studio

The Norton Commons studio sits inside Common Ground, the coworking space at the heart of Norton Commons in Prospect. The room is sound-treated and lit for video, with two armchairs, remote cameras and a switcher already rigged. Walk in, sit down and record.

The room

Inside Common Ground in Norton Commons. Warm, lamp-lit and sound-treated, with two cameras standing by.

  • RoomsSound-treated studio room inside Common Ground, seating for a host plus up to three guests
  • CamerasThree Panasonic remote-controlled PTZ cameras with a Blackmagic ATEM ISO switcher, individual feeds recorded
  • AudioSound-treated room, high-end dynamic microphones and professional audio interfaces
  • LightingProfessional lighting, rigged and ready — turn-key, no setup required
  • FilesBring a USB-C SSD and take raw audio and video away on the spot; raw audio can also be sent via Google Drive
  • BuildingInside Common Ground coworking; high-speed internet, parking, and a luxury Airbnb upstairs for travelling guests
