The books

Blood Stained Pews

What if the church became more than a home for the hypocrites? What if the church became a hospital to heal the hurting?

Buy the book Free group study

The premise

Jesus built a hospital. We keep running a waiting room.

Blood Stained Pews is a chance to examine Jesus’ original intent for the church: a hospital for the broken.

Carl is clear that Christians have been getting this wrong. In the book he gives real steps to change our hearts, our practices, and eventually our churches through the power of open brokenness. Through personal stories and hard-won insight, he asks us to take God’s grace more seriously than we do, and to turn our churches into the places people run to when they are wounded.

It is written for people who have been hurt by a church and have not given up on one, and for the leaders who would rather fix that than defend it.
