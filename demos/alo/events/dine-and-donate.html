How it works

  1. Pick a night

    Each partner hosts one date. Dine-in or carry-out, all day.

  2. Mention the ALO

    Tell your server, or show the flyer on your phone. Some partners count every cover that night regardless.

  3. The restaurant sends the share

    The agreed percentage goes to the association, and from there to the orchestra.

Own a restaurant?

Dine & Donate costs a partner nothing up front and brings a room full of people who plan their evenings around the arts. The association handles promotion to roughly 350 members and its mailing list; you set the date and the percentage.
