Rules & eligibility

  • Entrants must be 21 or older and legally able to receive alcohol in their state of residence.
  • Winners are drawn at random from all sold ticket numbers on Saturday, December 19, 2026.
  • You do not need to be present at the drawing to win.
  • Prizes must be collected in person in Louisville, with valid photo ID. Bourbon is not shipped.
  • Tickets are non-refundable once the drawing has taken place.
  • Employees of the association's licensed gaming operation and their immediate households are ineligible.
  • Ticket purchases are not tax-deductible charitable contributions.
  • Conducted under Kentucky charitable gaming licence EXE0002394.
