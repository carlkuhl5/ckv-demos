Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from patients, caregivers, and referral partners.

Many of our rides are billed through Medicaid transportation brokers and select insurance plans. Coverage varies by plan, so give us a call and we'll help you figure out what's available for your situation.

For routine appointments, we recommend booking at least 24–48 hours ahead. We understand medical needs come up fast, though — call us directly for same-day and urgent requests and we'll do everything we can to help.

We ask for as much notice as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule, ideally at least 2 hours before pickup, so we can offer that time slot to another patient. Call or text us any time to make a change.

Yes. Our fleet includes ramp- and lift-equipped vehicles with certified wheelchair lockdown systems, alongside standard vehicles for ambulatory riders.

We proudly serve Jefferson County and the greater Louisville metro area, including Oldham, Bullitt, and Shelby counties. Visit our Service Area page for the full list, or just give us a call to confirm your address.

We do our best to accommodate same-day requests, especially for dialysis and treatment appointments. Availability isn't guaranteed, so please call as early as possible.

In most vehicles, yes — there's room for one companion to ride along at no extra charge. Let us know when you book so we can plan accordingly.

All BMT drivers complete background screening, driving-record checks, and passenger-assistance training before they're on the road, and ongoing training after that.
