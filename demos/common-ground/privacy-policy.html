Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us through commongroundlou.com. Last updated 2026-11-12.

For client and legal review. This policy was rewritten to match how the new site actually works — forms, analytics and the member portal. Please have it checked before launch, and tell us if the current Squarespace policy contains commitments we should carry over.

Who we are

Common Ground is a co-working space at 12100 River Beauty Loop, Prospect, KY 40059. You can reach us at info@ourcommonground.co or (502) 790-7802. This policy covers the website at commongroundlou.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit a tour request, a room or event request, or a contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, and whatever you write in the message, along with your answers to the form's questions.
  • Usage information. If you accept analytics, we collect anonymised information about which pages are visited, how people arrive at the site, and roughly where in the world they are. We do not collect anything that identifies you personally through analytics.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and arrange tours, bookings and memberships.
  • To send you news about Common Ground, but only if you ticked the box asking us to.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful so we can improve them.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Cookies and analytics

The site shows a notice the first time you visit. Analytics only run if you accept. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is stored in your browser so we don't ask again, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Our member portal

Membership sign-up, billing and member login are handled by our member-management platform, which is a separate service with its own privacy policy. When you click "Sign up" or "Member login" you leave commongroundlou.com and that platform's terms apply.

Other services we link to

Pages on this site embed a Google Map and link out to Instagram, our tour calendar and our member portal. Those services set their own cookies and have their own policies.

How long we keep things

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to serve you, and then for a reasonable period for our records. Ask us to delete your information and we will, unless we're required to keep it.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Unsubscribe from any email we send — every one has a link.
  • Decline analytics in the cookie notice.

Email info@ourcommonground.co for any of these.

Children

This site is intended for adults and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of the page.
