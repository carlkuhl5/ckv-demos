How it works

Three steps, one room, done.

Pick a room and a time

Tell us which studio, what you want to record and roughly when. We come back with times that work and confirm the room.

Show up and record

Both rooms are rigged and lit before you arrive. Someone is on site the whole time. Bring a USB-C SSD and you can walk out with your raw files.

We cut it and you publish

Long form gets edited with your intro and thumbnail. Clips come back ready for social. Add distribution and we post them for you.
