About

Faith first, family second, company third.

In that order, on purpose. Here's where I come from and how I run things.

The foundation

Jesus is my foundation.

That isn't a line on a website for me — it's the thing everything else sits on. I serve in Kids Ministry at my church, and before that I led youth groups for nine years.

Nine years of standing in front of teenagers teaches you a few things about leading grown adults. You learn that people don't follow a title, they follow whoever is consistent. You learn that the standard you walk past is the standard you set. And you learn that teaching people is the most useful thing you can do with a day.

That's the same reason I coach contractors now.

Where I come from

My dad built kitchens.

I'm blue collar, start to finish. I grew up watching my dad build kitchens — real work, with his hands, that you could stand in front of when it was done. Nobody in my family handed me a business.

I started Beltway Heating, Air & Plumbing from nothing. No partners, no investors, no safety net. Eight years later it's 55 people and over $11 million, and I still own all of it.

I'm not telling you that to brag. I'm telling you because when I say I know what it's like to be the owner who's doing payroll at 11pm and sales calls at 7am, I actually mean it.

Family

The reason any of it matters.

My wife and my kids are why I build anything. The picture at the top of this site was taken at our office, in front of the logo, because the company and the family aren't two separate things around here.

A business that takes you away from the people you built it for isn't a business worth having. That's one of the first things I'll tell you on a call.

How I lead

Culture is your biggest KPI.

Everybody tracks revenue. Almost nobody tracks whether people want to be here. One of those predicts the other.

A players

Hire people who are better than you at their job, then get out of their way. One A player beats three who are just filling a seat.

High standards

The standard isn't what you say in a meeting. It's what you let slide on a Tuesday.

Thinking outside the box

Most of this trade does it the way it's always been done. That's exactly why there's room.

Branding

A truck people recognize is a salesperson that works while you sleep. We spent on brand early and it paid.

Community outreach

Show up in the place you make your money. It isn't marketing, but it works like it.

A love of teaching

Nine years with youth groups, now Kids Ministry, now coaching owners. Same job, different room.

From the podcast

Seven things I keep coming back to.

If you heard me on a podcast, this is the short version.

Humility over ego

Being talented or successful shouldn't make someone think they're above the team.

Character

Who someone is matters, not just how well they perform their job.

Company culture

Building a workplace people genuinely want to belong to is a priority.

Team chemistry

How employees work together matters alongside their individual abilities.

Resilience

Difficult circumstances aren't necessarily a reason to give up.

Self-belief

Being willing to take calculated personal risks to build something meaningful.

Pride in the company

Creating something employees are proud to represent, not simply a business that generates revenue.
