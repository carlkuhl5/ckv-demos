About

Husband, dad, church planter.

Carl Kuhl is a preacher and author whose life calling is to help broken people find hope in Jesus.

His story

The longer version.

Carl is married to Lindsay. Their favorite things to do together are traveling and trying new restaurants. He loves the Rocky Mountains, watching sports — Ravens football preferably — and working out. His biggest adventure is raising four great kids: Reagan, Quint, London, and Declan.

In 2008 Carl and Lindsay started Mosaic Christian Church. It grew into one of the hundred fastest-growing churches in the country, and Carl led it for fourteen years. In 2022 they relocated to Louisville, where he served as one of the executive pastors and as teaching pastor at Southeast Christian Church — fourteen campuses and thirty thousand people on a weekend.

He recently stepped down from that role. He is looking for his next leadership role, where he can build a culture in which broken people find endless second chances in Jesus.

He did not write a book about church hurt because he gave up on the church. He wrote it because he has spent his whole life building them.

Blood Stained Pews came out of that conviction: that Jesus meant the church to be a hospital for the hurting, and that most of us have quietly built something more comfortable instead. His second book, Church Planting For The Rest of Us, is for leaders who do not fit the profile — which is how he felt when he started.

What he cares about

Four things, consistently.

01

People the church has hurt

Not as a group to win back, but as people owed a straight answer. Most of his pastoral energy goes here.

02

Planters who do not fit the profile

He did not fit it either. He coaches a handful of them at a time and wrote a book for the rest.

03

Churches without a budget for any of this

Every manuscript and study on this site is free, because the churches that need the material most are usually the ones that cannot pay for it.

04

Second chances, past the point it gets awkward

Fourth one, ninth one. It is the thing he preaches most and the thing he wants a church to be known for.

Long bio  ·  ~250 words

Carl Kuhl is a husband, dad, church planter, preacher, and author whose life calling is to help broken people find hope in Jesus.

In 2008 Carl and his wife Lindsay started Mosaic Christian Church. Over the next fourteen years it became one of the hundred fastest-growing churches in the country. In 2022 the family relocated to Louisville, Kentucky, where Carl served as one of the executive pastors and as a teaching pastor at Southeast Christian Church, which has fourteen campuses and around thirty thousand people attending each weekend. He recently stepped down from that role and is looking for his next leadership position, where he can build a culture in which broken people find endless second chances in Jesus.

Carl is the author of Blood Stained Pews, which asks what would happen if the church became less a home for the hypocrites and more a hospital to heal the hurting, and Church Planting For The Rest of Us, written for leaders who do not fit the usual description of a church planter. He guest preaches at churches, conferences, and camps around the country, and coaches pastors and planters one on one.

Carl and Lindsay love to travel and try new restaurants. He is happiest in the Rocky Mountains, in the gym, or watching the Ravens. Their biggest adventure is raising four kids: Reagan, Quint, London, and Declan.
