The space

Every coworking space offers a place to work. We wanted to create something more — a floor you actually want to spend the day on, rooms that are ready when you book them, and a studio most spaces don't have.

What's here

24/7 member access

Badge in whenever you work best — early mornings, late nights, weekends.

Coffee & refreshments

Complimentary coffee and refreshments in the member kitchen.

Lightning-fast internet

High-speed Wi-Fi across every floor and every room.

Large & small conference rooms

Book a room for two or a room for a full team meeting.

Wireless screen sharing

Airtame casting in the conference rooms — no dongle hunt.

Fully equipped podcast studio

Flexible workspaces

Lounge seating, high-tops, quiet corners and focus rooms.

Zone by zone

Six places to work, depending on the day

Seating is unassigned, so you can move with the work — heads-down in the morning, a room at eleven, the lounge in the afternoon.

01

The main floor

Open coworking under the brick feature wall: a communal stone high-top for heads-down work, cafe tables for two, and lounge seating when you want to spread out. Unassigned, so you pick the spot that suits the day.

  • Communal high-top seating
  • Cafe tables for one or two
  • Lounge and sofa seating
  • Natural light on three sides

02

Lounge & cafe seating

Banquettes and marble tables along the windows — the easy place to take a call, meet a client informally, or work somewhere softer than a desk.

  • Banquette and sofa seating
  • Marble cafe tables
  • Steps from the coffee bar
  • Good for informal client meetings

03

Focus rooms

Small private rooms for calls and deep work, with a desk, monitor and a door that closes. Grab one when you need quiet without booking a conference room.

  • Desk and monitor
  • A door that closes
  • Built for calls and focus time
  • First come, first served

04

Conference rooms

Large and small conference rooms, both open to members and non-members. Each includes a TV display with HDMI, a whiteboard and Airtame wireless screen sharing.

  • TV / display with HDMI
  • Whiteboard
  • Airtame wireless casting
  • High-speed Wi-Fi

05

Event & workshop space

The main floor can be set for workshops, trainings, networking events, business meetings and other professional gatherings — for members and for outside organizations.

  • Workshops and trainings
  • Networking events
  • Team offsites
  • Nonprofit and educator sessions

06

Member kitchen

Complimentary coffee and refreshments, a full-size refrigerator and counter space — so a long day in the space doesn't mean leaving for lunch.

  • Complimentary coffee
  • Refreshments
  • Refrigerator and sink
  • Counter and prep space
