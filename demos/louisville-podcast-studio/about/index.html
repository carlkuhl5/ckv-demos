About

A place where small businesses and brands can thrive.

Louisville Podcast Studio was not born as a business idea. It was born out of necessity.

Cameron Becht · Owner

Hey, I’m Cameron.

Louisville Podcast Studio wasn’t born as a business idea—it was born out of necessity.

I needed a place where I could truly scale. A space built for growth. A studio where my brand, my voice, and my vision could expand without friction. So I created LPS as my own private studio—a system designed to make showing up consistently and powerfully effortless.

But once I opened those doors, something became clear. I wasn’t the only one who needed this.

I saw entrepreneurs, creators, and business owners all around me struggling with the same problem: they had something valuable to say, but no streamlined way to say it at scale. No single place that could shoot their content, edit it professionally, and distribute it with intention.

That’s when LPS evolved.

Through this exact process, I’ve grown an audience of over 400,000 followers across social platforms and generated more than $500,000 directly from social media. I saw firsthand what happens when long-form conversations meet short-form attention. What a single podcast can turn into. What a few strategic reels can unlock. Momentum. Authority. Opportunity.

LPS exists to give that same system to the community—to remove the friction, shorten the learning curve, and help business owners turn their message into real results.

This isn’t just a studio. It’s a launchpad.

My mission is simple: to help those who aspire to be great actually become it.

Cameron Becht, founder of Louisville Podcast Studio.
