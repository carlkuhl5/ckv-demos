How he works

Tell him the text and the room.

Carl does not travel with one talk. If you are in a series, he will preach your passage and your direction. If you want him to bring something, he will.

He has preached weekend services at Southeast, Mosaic, Traders Point, Flatirons, Lifepoint and elsewhere, on everything from Nehemiah to mental illness to what grace looks like on somebody’s fourth try. The sermon library is the fastest way to hear what a weekend with him actually sounds like.

He also coaches a small number of pastors and church planters one on one. That is usually a standing call rather than a one-off, so ask on the form and he will tell you honestly whether he has room.
