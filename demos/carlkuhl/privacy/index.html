Privacy policy

Short version: this site collects the minimum it needs to answer you, and it does not sell anything about you to anyone.

Last updated: [ DATE ]

What this site collects

  • Form submissions. When you submit the speaking, leadership, contact, or email-signup form, the details you type are sent to Carl and stored by the form provider.
  • Email subscriptions. If you subscribe, your address is stored by the email platform along with the date you confirmed.
  • Analytics. Aggregate, privacy-respecting page analytics — which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors came from. No cross-site tracking, no advertising profiles, no cookies used to identify you personally.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • It does not run advertising trackers or third-party marketing pixels.
  • It does not require an account, and it does not store payment details — book purchases happen entirely on the retailer’s own site under their policy.

Confidential inquiries

Inquiries submitted through the leadership form are treated as confidential. They are not published, not added to any mailing list, and not shared outside the people handling them.

Embedded video

Sermon videos are hosted on YouTube and load only after you click play. Until you click, no YouTube script runs and no data is sent to them. Once you play a video, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email using the link at the bottom of it.
  • Ask for a copy of what is held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing hello@carlkuhl.org.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
