Our Transportation Services

Every ride type below is available across our Louisville, KY service area. Not sure which fits your needs? Just call — we’ll help you figure it out.

Wheelchair-Accessible Transport

Ramp- and lift-equipped vehicles with secure wheelchair lockdowns for a smooth, dignified ride.

Ambulatory Transport

Comfortable door-to-door rides for patients who can walk or transfer with minimal assistance.

Stretcher Transport

Non-emergency stretcher transport available on request for patients unable to sit upright.

Dialysis Appointments

Dependable, recurring rides to and from dialysis treatment — built around your schedule.

Chemotherapy & Oncology Visits

Patient, compassionate transport for chemo and ongoing cancer treatment appointments.

Doctor & Specialist Visits

On-time rides to primary care, specialist, and follow-up appointments across the metro area.

Adult Day Care Transport

Consistent daily or weekly transportation to and from adult day programs.

Outpatient Surgery Transport

Pre- and post-procedure rides so you never have to arrange a ride from a hospital bed.

Social Outings & Errands

Rides for errands, visits, and social outings that keep patients connected and independent.

Equipment & Vehicles

Fully Equipped for Every Mobility Level

  • Ramp- and lift-equipped vans with certified wheelchair lockdowns.
  • Standard sedans and vans for ambulatory patients.
  • Stretcher-capable vehicles available on request.
  • Climate-controlled, regularly sanitized interiors.
