Who he is

Carl has spent his whole ministry building churches where broken people can stay.

He and Lindsay planted Mosaic Christian Church in 2008. It became one of the fastest-growing churches in the country. In 2022, after handing Mosaic off to his successor, Carl moved to Louisville, where he served as an executive pastor and teaching pastor at Southeast Christian Church.

He wrote Blood Stained Pews because he believes the church was meant to be a hospital, and too often it is a waiting room for people who already have it together. He preaches like he believes that. Bold, warm, and specific about what to do on Monday.

The book

Blood Stained Pews

What if the church became more than a home for the hypocrites? What if the church became a hospital to heal the hurting?

Carl is clear that Christians have been getting this wrong. Blood Stained Pews goes back to what Jesus intended the church to be, and gives real steps to change our hearts, our practices, and eventually our churches through the power of open brokenness.
