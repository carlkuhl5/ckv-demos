A la Carte 2026–27

About thirty evenings a year, hosted in members' own homes and underwritten by the people hosting them. Every ticket dollar goes to the Louisville Orchestra.

What A la Carte is

A la Carte began in 1987 and has run every season since. Board members and friends of the association sponsor and underwrite each event outright — the food, the wine, the flowers, the house. Because the evening is already paid for, the entire price of your ticket is donated to the Louisville Orchestra.

These are not fundraising galas in a ballroom. They are dinners for twenty in somebody's dining room, a shrimp boil on the Ohio, a murder mystery where you are given a character a week ahead. They sell out, and the small ones sell out first.

Before you book

  • 100% of the ticket price is donated to the Louisville Orchestra.
  • Approximately 50% is tax-deductible — the portion above the fair market value of what you receive. The exact deductible amount is stated on your receipt.
  • You do not have to be a member to attend, though members are notified first.
  • Addresses are private. Most events are in members' homes; the address arrives with your confirmation.

What changed

Twelve events, twelve prices, one button each. On the current site none of the twelve shows a price; the buttons alternate between “Buy Tickets” and “Details” with no discernible rule; the two undated events sit inline between real dates; and the dates carry no year. Every one of those is a decision a visitor has to make on the organisation's behalf before they can decide whether to come.

Host an evening instead

The hospitality committee brings the plan, the volunteers, and the guest list. You bring the house and pick the date.
