The podcast studio

A fully equipped studio on site at Common Ground — for businesses, creators, educators and organizations who want to sound like they know what they're doing.

Ask about the studioSee the rest of the space

What's on site

A real room, not a corner with a microphone

Our professional podcast studio sits inside Common Ground, so you can record without booking a separate venue across town. Members get a discounted rate, and the studio is available to non-members too.

It's used for podcasts, recorded interviews, video content, training material and internal comms — the kind of thing that used to mean hiring a crew.

  • Fully equipped podcast studio
  • A private room with a door that closes
  • High-speed internet for remote guests
  • Coffee, refreshments and a lounge for guests

The lounge outside the studio — where guests wait and the post-record conversation happens.

Who it's for

Four kinds of people book this room

Businesses

Customer interviews, thought-leadership series, recruiting content and internal updates that don't look like they were filmed on a laptop.

Creators

A reliable weekly room for a show, with space for guests and somewhere decent for them to sit beforehand.

Educators

Course modules, lecture capture and training material recorded in one afternoon instead of five.

Nonprofits

Donor stories, campaign videos and board updates — told properly, without a production budget.

Looking for Louisville Podcast Studio?

Louisville Podcast Studio operates its own studio in Norton Commons. It's a separate business from Common Ground — if that's who you came here for, you'll find them at louisvillepodcaststudio.com.

If you'd rather record in our studio, or you want both the studio and a workspace, we can help with that.
