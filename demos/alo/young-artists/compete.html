Rules & eligibility

Everything the flyer says, in text you can search, read on a phone, and link a teacher straight to.

Eligibility

Students must be in grades 5–12 and currently studying with a teacher in Kentucky, or in Indiana and Ohio counties within a radius of 125 miles of Louisville.

Repertoire

The music performed must be a memorised concerto of your choice and must not exceed 15 minutes. If the movement is longer, apply appropriate cuts in the tutti sections. Applicants play the entire movement in both the preliminaries and the finals.

Orchestral accompaniment must require no fewer than 15 orchestra musicians, or the entire string section.

Each applicant provides their own accompanist and lists the accompanist's name on the application at the time of submission. Applicants must provide an extra copy of the music for the judges; it is returned after the competition.

The rounds

Preliminaries — Saturday, November 7, 2026

Judges select a maximum of three finalists from each division. Each finalist receives a $50 award, a voucher for two tickets to a Louisville Orchestra Classics concert, and a participant certificate.

Audience: only the student's teacher and accompanist may be present.

Finals recital — Saturday, November 21, 2026

Held at the University of Louisville School of Music and open to the public. Judges select a maximum of three winners.

Awards

  • First place — a Performance Award, an invitation to perform as guest soloist with the Louisville Orchestra, $750 cash, a voucher for two Classics Concert season series, and a finalist certificate.
  • Second and third place — $500 cash, a voucher for two Classics Concert season series, and a finalist certificate.
  • Preliminary finalists — $50 and two Classics Concert tickets.

Future eligibility

All applicants, if otherwise eligible, may compete in the same or a higher division the following year according to their new grade in school. A Performance Award cannot be given to the same student in two successive years in the same division.

Entry fee

$30 per division, paid as part of the application. Entering two divisions costs $60 and is one application, not two.

Key dates

  • Applications openAugust 15, 2026
  • DeadlineOctober 16, 2026
  • PreliminariesNovember 7, 2026
  • Finals recitalNovember 21, 2026

Divisions close early if they reach 20 entrants. Priority is by receipt of a completed application.

Apply — $30 per division
