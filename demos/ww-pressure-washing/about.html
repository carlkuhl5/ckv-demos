About

Local, insured, and easy to get hold of

Bringing your property back to life — that is not a slogan we bought, it is the actual job.

Why we started W&W

Exterior cleaning is one of the few home services where the result is completely obvious. Either the driveway looks different when you pull in or it does not. That is a good business to be judged on, and it is why we started this one.

W&W Pressure Washing is locally owned and operated here in Southern Indiana. We are newer as a company, which we would rather say plainly than dress up. What that means in practice is that every single job matters more to us than it would to an outfit with a hundred of them booked — we are building the reputation right now, on your driveway.

It also means we are careful about scope. We do driveways, walkways, and gutters, and we do them with the right equipment for each surface. We are not going to talk you into a roof wash we are not set up to do properly. When we add a service, it will be because we can stand behind it.

How we work

  • Quotes go out fast — usually the same day, and you can get one from photos without an in-person visit.
  • We tell you up front what will fully lift and what will only lighten.
  • Pressure and chemistry get matched to the surface, every time.
  • Before and after photos on every job, whether you were home or not.
  • Fully insured, with a certificate available whenever you want to see it.
