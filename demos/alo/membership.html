Membership

About 350 people who decided the Louisville Orchestra was worth a few evenings a year. Levels start at $30.

Join or renew

What it means to be a member

Membership dues go to the Louisville Orchestra. That is the first and simplest thing it means: you are partnering with the orchestra to keep the music playing, and the money moves.

The second thing it means is optional, and it is the reason most members stay. The association runs on volunteers — the parties are hosted by members, the competition is staffed by members, the newsletter is written by a member. You can join, pay dues, come to two dinners a year and never do another thing. Or you can pick a committee on the way in and find yourself, three years later, running a murder mystery for forty people.

How members fulfil the mission

  • Host or attend fundraising events — A la Carte, Dine & Donate, the raffle.
  • Organise, staff, and sponsor the Young Artist Competition.
  • Attend Louisville Orchestra concerts and advocate for the orchestra in the community.

What dues fund

Dues are the association's only unrestricted income. Everything else — ticket sales, the raffle, Dine & Donate — is raised against a specific event. Dues pay for the things that have no event attached to them.

  • Prize money for the Young Artist Competition
  • Instruments refurbished for the instrument playground
  • Musician lunches at MakingMusic school concerts
  • The newsletter, the roster, and this website

Dues are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. The association is a registered 501(c)(3); EIN 61-6027223.

Five levels

What it costs

These five prices appear once on this site, on one page, with one checkout behind them.

Student

$30 / year

For students through college, at any level of involvement.

  • Full member voting rights
  • Member pricing on every A la Carte event
  • Season newsletter and event announcements
Join at $30

Senior

$40 / year

For members 65 and over. Everything in Individual.

  • Full member voting rights
  • Member pricing on every A la Carte event
  • Season newsletter and event announcements
  • Invitation to the annual meeting
Join at $40

Individual

$50 / year

One membership, one name on the roster.

  • Full member voting rights
  • Member pricing on every A la Carte event
  • Season newsletter and event announcements
  • Invitation to the annual meeting
  • Volunteer placement on the committee of your choice
Join at $50

Household

$75 / year

Two adults at one address, both on the roster.

  • Everything in Individual, for two people
  • Two votes at the annual meeting
  • Member pricing on every A la Carte event for both members
  • One mailing, not two
Join at $75

OPEN — the live site calls this both “Family” and “Membership Plus One.” Whether $75 covers two named adults or a whole household is a kickoff decision.

Lifetime

$750 once

Paid once. Membership for life, and a permanent line in the association's record.

  • Everything in Individual, permanently
  • No renewal notice, ever
  • Recognition in the annual meeting program
  • Named in the Lifetime Members roll
Join at $750
What changed

The current site sells membership on two pages that disagree with each other and do not link to one another. /become-a-member offers “Member $50 / Membership Plus One $75 / Lifetime $750”; /plans-pricing offers “Student $30 / Senior Citizen $40 / Individual $50 / Family $75 / Life $750” — and a third path sells an annual meeting ticket and the competition fee from the same pricing table. A prospective member comparing the two pages cannot tell what this organisation charges. Exactly one URL sells membership here (PRD Section 07).
