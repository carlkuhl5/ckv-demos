Murder in Glitter Ball City

A 1970s murder mystery dinner where every guest gets a character, a secret, and a reason to lie about it.

Costumes encouraged, alibis required

The evening

You get your character and your secret a week ahead. Disco, dinner, and a body by the second course. Costumes are encouraged and consistently excellent. The person who solves it wins a prize; the person who commits it wins the evening.
