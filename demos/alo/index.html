The mission

What the association actually does

Three things, stated the way the bylaws state them — and each one has a page that shows the work rather than describing it.

Raising funds

About thirty A la Carte parties, dinners, and outings a year, every one of them underwritten by a board member or a friend of the association — so the full ticket price goes to the orchestra, not to overhead.

See the season

Encouraging youth

The Young Artist Competition has run since 1944. Winners perform as guest soloists with the Louisville Orchestra. The association also funds MakingMusic concerts and the instrument playground at family concerts.

About the competition

Broadening audiences

Roughly 350 members who attend concerts, bring guests who have never been, and advocate for the orchestra across the region. Membership starts at $30.
