Terms & refunds

What you are buying, and what happens if plans change.

Last updated September 4, 2026.

Memberships

Annual memberships run for one year from the date of purchase. Lifetime memberships do not expire. Dues are a charitable contribution to a registered 501(c)(3) and are generally non-refundable; if you joined at the wrong level, contact membership@associationlo.org within 14 days and we will correct it rather than refund and re-charge.

A la Carte tickets

Tickets are refundable up to 14 days before the event. Inside 14 days the host has already committed to catering and numbers, so tickets become non-refundable — but they are transferable, and we will happily change the name on the host's list. If the association cancels an event, every ticket is refunded in full.

Approximately 50% of an A la Carte ticket price is tax-deductible; the remainder represents the fair market value of the evening. The exact deductible amount is stated on your receipt.

Donations

Donations are non-refundable. Monthly gifts can be cancelled at any time from the link in your receipt, and take effect before the next charge. No goods or services are provided in exchange for a donation, so the full amount is deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Young Artist Competition entries

The $30 per-division entry fee is refundable for withdrawals made before the application deadline of October 16, 2026. After the deadline the judging panel and venue are committed and the fee is non-refundable. Refunds are issued from the association's admin, not by cheque from a volunteer.

Raffle tickets

Conducted under Kentucky charitable gaming licence EXE0002394. Entrants must be 21 or older. Tickets are non-refundable once the drawing has taken place. Raffle ticket purchases are not tax-deductible charitable contributions. Prizes are collected in person in Louisville with valid photo identification and are not shipped. Full rules appear on the raffle page and are acknowledged at checkout.

Payments

All payments are processed by Stripe. The association does not store card numbers. Charges appear on your statement as the Association of the Louisville Orchestra.

Contact

Questions about any of the above: info@associationlo.org or (502) 893-5843.
