Board & officers

The association is run entirely by volunteers. These are the 14 officers and 7 directors who run it this year.

What changed

Leadership is a record type here, not a block of pasted text: name, role, committee, photo, bio, and term year. Publishing next year's board is a form the membership chair fills in, and the contact routing on every page updates with it. Two entries below are reproduced exactly as the current site publishes them — the same person listed as both Immediate Past President and VP Communications — and flagged for confirmation at kickoff rather than quietly corrected.

Volunteer with a committee

Members choose their committee interests when they join, and the chair of that committee gets the name the same day.
