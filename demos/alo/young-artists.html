The Young Artist Competition

Run every fall since 1944. First place performs as guest soloist with the Louisville Orchestra.

Apply — $30 per division Rules & eligibility

Applications close Friday, October 16, 2026.

What it is

Every fall, musicians in grades 5–12 who study within 125 miles of Louisville play a memorised concerto movement for a panel of judges. Three finalists come out of each division. From the finals, the judges choose a first-place winner who is invited to perform as guest soloist with the Louisville Orchestra.

The competition has run since 1944 — the year the association was founded. It is the clearest public good this organisation does, and for a serious student in this region it is frequently the first time they play a concerto with an orchestra behind them.

The dates that matter

  • Applications open — Saturday, August 15, 2026
  • Deadline — Friday, October 16, 2026, or when a division fills at 20 entrants, whichever comes first
  • Preliminaries — Saturday, November 7, 2026
  • Finals recital — Saturday, November 21, 2026, University of Louisville School of Music, open to the public

Also for young people

The rest of the youth programme

The competition is the visible part. These run alongside it, and members volunteer at all of them.

MakingMusic concerts

Louisville Orchestra concerts for fourth and fifth graders across the region. The association provides lunches for the musicians so the orchestra can play two school concerts in a day instead of one.

The instrument playground

At family concerts, children get their hands on real instruments — refurbished or bought outright by the association — before or after the music. Members staff it.

Pre-concert activities

Craft tables, instrument petting, and a first introduction to a concert hall for children whose families have never been inside one.
