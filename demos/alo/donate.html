Give

Every dollar goes to the Louisville Orchestra and to music education for young people in this region. Give any amount, once or every month.

Make a gift

1. How much

Any amount. You will never be told to ring a volunteer to give a number that isn't on a list.

2. Where it goes

Your designation appears on your receipt and in the treasurer's export, so it is honoured rather than remembered.

3. Remember with Music

They receive a note that a gift was made in this person's name. The amount is never disclosed.

Your gift

    Total per month $0
    Fully tax-deductible. The Association of the Louisville Orchestra is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. EIN 61-6027223. No goods or services are provided in exchange for a donation, so the entire amount is deductible to the extent allowed by law.

    Prefer to send a check?

    Make it payable to ALO and mail to:

    Attn: Treasurer
    Association of the Louisville Orchestra
    624 W Main St, Suite 400
    Louisville, KY 40202

    Addressed to the role, not the person — so this stays correct when the treasurer changes.

    What changed

    The current donate page has no giving levels, no tax-deductibility statement, no EIN, and no tribute option — despite “Remember with Music” being a named programme on the about page. Its instruction for a custom amount is, verbatim: “If you do not see the amount you want to donate, Ann can help. Send her a message at Contact Us and she will reach back to you with a call.” A donor who wants to give $250 is asked to book a phone call. The check-by-mail instructions are addressed to a named volunteer, so the page breaks when the treasurer changes.

    Or make it recurring

    A monthly gift is the most useful kind an organisation this size can receive — it is the money the board can plan against. Toggle it above; cancel any time from your own receipt.
